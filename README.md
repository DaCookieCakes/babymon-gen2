# BabyMon
### A mod to add cut baby pokemon for gen1recomp++! (gen 2)
All baby pokemon grow through happiness, though evolution is currently untested.

The list is as follows:
* Bipulla  (Girafarig)
* Burgela  (Tangela)
* Coinpur  (Meowth)
* Dodaerie (Doduo)
* Kolta    (Ponyta)
* Orfry    (Goldeen)
* Paraspor (Paras)
* Pupperon (Growlithe)
* Smujj    (Grimer)
* Vulpiii  (Vulpix)

# prerequisites
* [Pokemon Gen1Recomp++](<https://github.com/bryanthaboi/gen1recomp>) by Bryanthaboi
* [Expanded Species](<https://github.com/mistermiracle3036/Expanded-Species>) by Mistermiracle

# Name Translations
### The translations come from Nob Ogasawara himself.
* Bipulla  | Bipolar, and Puller
* Burgela  | (Tan)gela, Bur (seed), and Burgeoning (to grow/flourish)
* Coinpur  | Coin Purse, and Purr
* Dodaerie | Dod(uo/rio), and Aerie (bird nest/family of birds)
* Kolta    | (Pony)ta, Coal, and Colt
* Orfry    | (Gold) Ore, and Fry (baby fish)
* Paraspor | Paras, and Spore
* Pupperon | Pup, and Pepperoni
* Smujj    | Smudge
* Vulpiii  | Vulp(ix), and Roman Numeral 3 (III) | Nob wishes they could rename Vulpix and Ninetails to "Vulpvi" and "Vulpix".
